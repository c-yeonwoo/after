# Design System: After

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Orange Companion"**

After pairs vivid orange action with clear neutral surfaces and a detailed adult assistant character. Short Korean titles explain the member's actual progress; compact controls and comfortable conversational text keep the next action easy to find. Korean, Latin text, and numbers share the self-hosted Wanted Sans family.

White is the light canvas and neutral charcoal is the dark canvas. The same orange remains recognizable in both themes, with dark ink on its filled controls. Small assistant avatars identify the speaker beside conversational content; human profile photography remains contextual to the person being introduced.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Vivid orange actions and selections with dark ink.
- Neutral assistant speech, a white light canvas, and a charcoal dark canvas.
- Detailed adult character avatars beside assistant messages.
- A mobile frame with stable chrome and an independently scrolling body.
- Brief tactile feedback that respects reduced motion.

This record extracts the implemented system from `src/styles.css`, `AppScreen`, `AssistantMark`, `AssistantDock`, the home route, profile preparation, conversation preparation, and shared controls. It records source evidence; it does not assert a completed browser or device QA pass.

## Colors

The frontmatter preserves resolved OKLCH values from the semantic CSS variables. The primary fill corresponds to the approved vivid orange (`#FF5A00`); dark-prefixed entries record theme overrides. Runtime components continue to use semantic CSS variables.

### Primary

- **Vivid Orange** (`primary`, `selection`, `bubble-mine`): main actions, selected controls, active navigation tiles, and the member's chat bubbles. These semantic roles intentionally share the same orange.
- **Orange Hover** (`primary-hover`): the filled action's dedicated hover state.
- **Readable Orange** (`primary-strong`): small links, brand text, caret, and the focus ring. It is deeper in light mode and lighter in dark mode.
- **Action Ink** (`primary-foreground`, `selection-foreground`): dark lettering on the orange fill in either theme.

### Neutral

- **White Canvas** (`background`, `card`): the light app ground and surface; dark mode separates charcoal ground from a slightly lighter surface.
- **Charcoal Ink** (`foreground`) and **Quiet Ink** (`muted-foreground`): primary and supporting text.
- **Neutral Speech** (`assistant-surface`, `assistant-ink`): pale neutral with dark ink in light mode, charcoal with light ink in dark mode.
- **Quiet Surface** (`muted`), **Soft Edge** (`border`), and **Field Edge** (`input`): quieter controls, structural edges, and visible field boundaries.
- **Raspberry**, **Green**, and **Amber** (`destructive`, `success`, `warning`): error, success, and warning meaning.

**The Same Orange Rule.** Keep the primary orange fill consistent across light and dark themes; use theme-specific neutral ink and the stronger text token for readability.

## Typography

**Display and Body Font:** Wanted Sans Variable, with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, and sans-serif fallbacks.

The official font's Unicode-range WOFF2 subsets are self-hosted with `font-display: swap`. Provenance and the SIL OFL license live in `public/fonts/wanted-sans/README.md` and `OFL.txt`. Mixed Korean, Latin, and numerals stay in the same family; Korean paragraphs use keep-all wrapping with emergency wrapping for long content.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** the profile-preparation welcome title.
- **Headline:** the short home state title and conversation-preparation title; the home title is constrained to 19rem.
- **Onboarding headline:** responsive question titles, using the existing clamp rather than a fixed headline size.
- **Title:** topic-card headings; chrome uses the same size with semibold weight and slightly tighter tracking.
- **Body / Supporting:** substantial human answers / assistant context and controls.
- **Label / Micro label:** navigation, status, captions, and chips. Both existing micro scale names resolve to 12px; their line heights differ.
- **Profile title / Introduction:** the established profile-specific size and weight variations.
- **Wordmark / Logo / Display wordmark:** existing brand utilities and the logo's tighter tracking; these are legitimate type treatments rather than new UI fonts.
- **Quote:** the existing `Gowun Batang`, `Nanum Myeongjo`, `AppleMyungjo`, serif stack and tighter tracking, reserved for the existing short quotation. Do not extend it to assistant UI.

The global tracking is the body token. The shared headline utility uses 700 weight, 1.25 line height, and -0.02em tracking; the explicit home headline uses its own documented headline role. Button defaults use supporting-size semibold text; the large button's 15px role is retained. Existing typography values are recorded to distinguish these intentional variants from accidental drift.

**The UI Family Rule.** Use Wanted Sans for assistant titles, questions, answers, controls, and numerals; retain the established serif quotation exception.

## Layout

The customer app is a centered mobile column with a maximum width of 430px. Its frame height is `calc(100dvh - var(--keyboard-height))`; the document does not scroll. Headers, footers, and bottom navigation remain in flex flow while the main body owns scrolling. Full conversation screens delegate scrolling to their message log. Admin layouts sit outside this mobile constraint.

Align headers, body, and footers to the gutter token. Use compact/related steps within a thought and section/separation steps between thoughts. The home begins with a short state title, then its current-state content. The confirmed appointment is a compact orange surface without an assistant portrait. The assistant dock follows with a small identity row, suggestions, optional reply, and composer.

Top safety space is `max(calc(env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px) + 0.5rem), 2.75rem)`; bottom safety space is `max(env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px), 2.125rem)`. At heights at or below 540px, conversation/form/onboarding screens relax these floors to the actual insets. Coarse-pointer fields retain at least 16px text. Onboarding hides its footer while the keyboard is open.

**The Stable Chrome Rule.** Put scrolling in the body and keep action areas in the app frame's flow; preserve the safe-area and keyboard calculations.

## Elevation & Depth

Tone carries most hierarchy. The card shadow token is `none`; the assistant dock now sits directly on the canvas without a surrounding card or shadow. Conversation-preparation topic surfaces retain the elevated shadow. Primary controls carry a small ambient shadow that disappears on press, and bottom navigation retains a soft upward shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Frame:** separates the centered app from a wide viewport, with its existing dark counterpart.
- **Elevated surface:** conversation-preparation topics and the shared elevation utility; not the assistant dock.
- **Primary control:** small control lift, removed on press.
- **Tab bar:** upward separation for stable navigation.
- **Chat composer:** the existing local composer lift.

Exact shadow and motion values live in the sidecar because the frontmatter schema does not support them. Shared press feedback uses 140ms and scales to 0.98. Assistant replies move 5px over 200ms; existing content arrival and active-tab motion use 260ms. Reduced motion suppresses the applicable arrival and press effects.

## Shapes

Controls and fields share the control radius; regular surfaces use the surface radius. The assistant avatar is circular. Status/welcome speech and profile questions retain directional lower-left corners; replies and profile answers retain their established asymmetric corners. These forms identify conversational turns without adding a decorative tail.

Circular back controls, progress tracks, and switch tracks remain functional exceptions to the standard control radius.

## Components

### Buttons

Tactile and clear. The six shared variants are primary, destructive, outline, secondary, ghost, and link. Default buttons have a 44px minimum target; large buttons have a 48px minimum target and 24px horizontal padding. Primary uses orange with dark ink. Outline uses a soft border, secondary uses the quiet surface, and ghost/link reduce emphasis.

Focus uses a visible 2px ring with a 2px offset. Shared disabled buttons switch to muted fill/text and remove shadow. Local controls such as the assistant send button retain their own implemented disabled treatment.

### Chips

Selected choices use orange fill, contrasting dark ink, semibold weight, and a check icon. Unselected choices use the card surface and soft border. Preserve the 44px minimum target and expose selection programmatically as implemented.

### Cards / Containers

The confirmed appointment uses orange, the surface radius, and gutter padding. It contains real appointment facts and a compact follow-up action, without an AI portrait. Home titles and cards derive from the real server state, including loading, error, and required-action states.

Conversation-preparation topics use the card surface and elevated shadow. Their evidence stays an indented quotation rather than a second nested card. Profile answers use directional corners and body text without a shadow. The generic legacy Card still combines a border and framework shadow; it does not define assistant dock styling.

### Inputs / Fields

Shared inputs use a 48px height, field radius, card surface, and visible field border. Focus shifts the border to the focus color and adds the ring/offset treatment. Invalid input uses raspberry; disabled inputs retain their existing reduced opacity.

The assistant composer is a bordered card-surface field with 6px outer padding, a transparent inline input, and a 44px orange submit control. Suggestions use a quiet neutral fill and 44px minimum target. Replies use neutral assistant speech colors and an asymmetric corner.

### Navigation

Two bottom destinations, 홈 and 소개, sit in a centered 18rem navigation row. Each tab has a 64px minimum height, 20px icon, and 12px label. The active icon occupies a 48 × 32px orange tile; its stroke strengthens from 1.7 to 2.3. Preserve the bottom inset.

On the home and introduction roots, 44px header controls expose account access and the shop for male members only, preserving the existing ticket restrictions. Direct member chat remains reachable through meeting detail and its existing opening conditions. Memory, scheduling, and assistant-chat production gates remain authoritative; visual navigation changes do not grant those capabilities.

### Assistant Signature

The default signature is a 40px circular crop of the user-approved detailed adult character asset. Female members see the male assistant asset; male members see the female assistant asset. The current asset paths are `/brand/concierge/male.webp` and `/brand/concierge/female.webp`. When member gender is unavailable, the existing MessageCircleHeart icon is the fallback. The avatar is decorative alongside the assistant's text identity and does not impersonate the counterpart.

Preserve the supplied detailed v2 character artwork. Cropping and sizing may serve the interface; do not redraw, simplify, stylize, or artistically transform the approved character. The profile-preparation welcome currently overrides the default mark to 56px; it is a local welcome treatment, not a larger appointment portrait.

The dock's identity row reads 애프터 AI. Its quick help and replies reflect actual member state. The composer remains private assistant input and follows the existing feature gates.

### Profile Question and Answer

Questions are compact neutral prompts at supporting size, 700 weight, and keep-all wrapping. Answers are offset card surfaces at body size/line height, preserve line breaks, and remain selectable.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Do use the same vivid orange fill and semantic theme variables across light and dark themes.
- Do preserve the supplied detailed adult assistant artwork and the member-to-assistant gender mapping.
- Do preserve visible keyboard focus, 44px control targets, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do keep the stable app chrome, safe areas, and keyboard-aware body scrolling.
- Do reuse self-hosted Wanted Sans and retain the existing short serif quotation exception.
- Do derive status titles and appointment facts from real state and retain existing capability gates.

### Don't

- Don't reintroduce purple, lilac, or pastel assistant speech as the system identity.
- Don't redraw, artistically simplify, or transform the approved detailed v2 character assets.
- Don't put an AI portrait into the compact appointment card or impersonate a counterpart's message.
- Don't introduce decorative eyebrow labels above already clear titles.
- Don't wrap supporting evidence in another card inside a topic card.
- Don't treat the legacy generic Card or a removed dock shadow as assistant defaults.
